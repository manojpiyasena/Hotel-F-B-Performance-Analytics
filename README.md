# How can hotel Food & Beverage management maximise restaurant revenue while controlling food and labour costs across different meal periods and guest segments?

Restaurant revenue analysis    
Food cost and labour cost    
Operational performance and recommendations

Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing hotel restaurant performance using Python, Pandas and data visualisation techniques. The objective is to understand how restaurant revenue, guest behaviour, occupancy levels, food costs and labour costs influence overall Food & Beverage operational performance.

The analysis examines restaurant revenue across different meal periods – Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner – to identify revenue trends, peak trading periods and opportunities to improve profitability and resource utilisation.

A key part of the project is understanding how different guest segments contribute to restaurant revenue and demand. These segments include Corporate Guests, Leisure Guests, Backpackers, Day Travellers and Groups.

By combining revenue, occupancy, guest segmentation, food cost and labour cost information, the project aims to identify operational patterns, highlight cost inefficiencies and support better commercial decision-making in hotel Food & Beverage operations.

Expected Business Value

The analysis aims to transform operational data into meaningful business insights that can help hotel management improve restaurant revenue, understand guest behaviour, manage food and labour costs, optimise staffing levels and make informed operational decisions.

This project combines hospitality operational knowledge with data analytics to demonstrate how Python and data visualisation can support commercially focused Food & Beverage management.
